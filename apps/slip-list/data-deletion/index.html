Data Deletion

How to erase everything Slip List has stored.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Slip List does not use accounts or sign-in, so there is no username, password, or server-side profile tied to you for us to delete. Every slip, archive entry, and setting lives only in local storage on your device — see the Privacy Policy for details.

Delete everything from inside the app

Open Slip List → Settings → Erase everything, then confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your current slip, your entire archive, and your saved preferences from the device.

Delete everything via Android system settings

Open Android Settings → Apps → Slip List → Storage, then tap Clear storage (or simply uninstall the app). This has the same effect as erasing from inside the app.

Nothing left on our side

Slip List has no server and no account system, so there is no remote copy of your to-do content to request deletion of. The app also does not show ads and does not collect an advertising ID, so there is no ad-network profile linked to your device either.
